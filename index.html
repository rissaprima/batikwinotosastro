Genuine Traditional
Hand-Waxed Process

Every line on our cloth is drawn by hand with hot wax, the way batik has been made in Yogyakarta. We keep the whole process alive.

See the process
The Hand-Waxed Way

Two ways to draw in wax.

Hot wax keeps the dye away from the cloth exactly where the artisan wants it. It can be drawn by hand with a canting, or stamped with a copper cap. Both take many days, and we keep every step.
